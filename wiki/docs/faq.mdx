# FAQ & troubleshooting

## Why won't it stack?

Look at the mob and run:

```
/mobstacker inspect
```

(or name one: `/mobstacker inspect @e[type=cow,limit=1,sort=nearest]`). It shows:

- the stack's size and health, the region it stands in and what that region sets,
- the size ceiling in force and where it comes from, and where the mob came from,
- **whether it can stack, and if not, the first rule that says no**: `stackMode`, a deny region, a mob
  list, its origin, a name, its gear, a player's claim on it, a full stack…
- and what happens between it and the nearest mob of its kind: they merge (and which one survives), or
  why not, down to "they differ in wool colour".

The game and the command read the same rules, so what it says is what happens.

## Common questions

**Nothing stacks at all.**
Stacking is off on a fresh install. Run `/mobstacker set stackMode everywhere` (or see
[Where mobs stack]($stacking/where_mobs_stack)). In `regions` mode, mobs only stack inside an **allow**
region.

**Mobs with armour or a weapon never stack.**
That is `stackEquippedMobs`, off by default. See [Equipment]($gameplay/equipment).

**My named pet does not stack.** / **My named stack stopped growing.**
A single mob renamed with a name tag is protected; a renamed stack keeps growing. See
[Names & colours]($stacking/names_and_colours#name-tags).

**Horses, wolves or cats I tamed do not stack.**
Anything a player has tamed, saddled, leashed or loaded never stacks. See
[Pets & mounts]($gameplay/pets_and_mounts).

**Bees, villagers, wandering traders or a boss do not stack.**
Since 1.12.0 some mobs stay single on purpose: the kinds in `#mobstacker:unstackable`, villagers with a
bed or a job, animals being bred, raiders in a raid, bosses unless `stackBosses` is on, and a few more.
See [Which mobs stack]($stacking/which_mobs_stack#never-whatever-the-lists-say).

**`maxstack list` says 64 but the config screen says 5000.**
Both are right: a mob type with its own ceiling ignores `maxStackSize`. See
[How stacks form]($stacking/how_stacks_form#how-big-a-stack-may-grow).

**A whole stack of sheared sheep grew its wool back at once.**
The stack shares one shear state, so one sheep eating grass regrows it for all of them.

**Fewer mobs spawn than in vanilla, even with stacking off.**
The mod lowers vanilla's spawn caps by default. See
[For server owners]($reference/server_owners#mob-caps).

**`/mobstacker` answers nothing.**
Your server has the gamerule `sendCommandFeedback` off. Turn on `alwaysShowFeedback`; see
[For server owners]($reference/server_owners#command-answers).

**Players cannot see a region.**
Regions are private by default: players see only the regions they own or that were shared with them.
See [Private regions]($regions/private_regions).

**The X-ray button is greyed out.**
The server has to allow it with the Java argument `-Dmobstacker.xray=true`. See
[Seeing regions]($regions/seeing_regions#through-walls-x-ray).

**Region boxes look wrong or are missing with a shader pack.**
Shader packs draw lines and see-through faces their own way. See
[Seeing regions]($regions/seeing_regions#shaders-and-modpacks).

**I want to remove the mod.**
Unstack first, or every stack keeps only one of its mobs. See
[Removing the mod]($reference/server_owners#removing-the-mod).

**Does it work with Let Me Despawn?**
Yes; it is a good companion for vanilla-style despawning, and so are spawn-control mods such as
In Control! or Spawncap Control Utility.

## Reporting a bug

Open an issue on [GitHub](https://github.com/michalekjarmark/mob-stacker/issues) with the mod version,
the loader (Fabric or Forge), your other mods, and the answer `/mobstacker inspect` gives for the
mob in question. For problems with region boxes, add the `[MobStacker overlay debug]` lines from
`logs/latest.log` (see [Seeing regions]($regions/seeing_regions#shaders-and-modpacks)).
