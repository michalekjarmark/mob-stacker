# Creating regions

A region is a box in one dimension, of one of two kinds:

| Kind | Effect |
|---|---|
| `allow` | Mobs may stack here, in the `regions` [stack mode]($stacking/where_mobs_stack). |
| `deny` | Mobs never form a stack here, in **every** mode. |

Both kinds can also carry [their own settings]($regions/region_settings), which apply to every mob
inside them. Shapes that are not a box are built from several regions.

## In the config screen

Open `/mobstackerconfig` → **Regions…**:

- **New region…** opens the area editor: a name, the kind, the dimension and both corners. **Here**
  fills a corner with the block you are standing on.
- **Pick in world…** lets you click the corners instead: right-click one block, then another. While
  you pick, the box from the first corner to the block you are looking at is drawn, so you see the
  reach before saving. Sneak and click to cancel. Nothing is placed on the blocks you click, and
  nothing is saved until you press **Save**.
- **Edit area…** reshapes or renames the selected region, and its **Delete** button asks for a second
  click.

A region keeps its corners the way you gave them: the first block you clicked is corner 1.

## With commands

Corners are block coordinates; `~` works as in any command. The region is created in the dimension
you are in.

```
/mobstacker region add <name> <allow|deny> <x1 y1 z1> <x2 y2 z2>
/mobstacker region add cowfarm allow ~-15 ~-3 ~-15 ~15 ~5 ~15
/mobstacker region bounds cowfarm 100 60 -40 140 75 0     # move or resize, keeps its settings
/mobstacker region type cowfarm deny
/mobstacker region rename cowfarm cows                   # keeps area, settings and colour
/mobstacker region list
/mobstacker region show cows                             # corners, priority and its own settings
/mobstacker region remove cows
```

> [!TIP]
> Adding an `allow` region while `stackMode` is `off` switches it to `regions`, so the new region works
> at once.

## Overlapping regions

Where regions overlap, **the one with the higher priority** gives the settings, and a tie goes to the
**smaller** region. Priority is 0 unless you set it:

```
/mobstacker region priority cows 10
```

Whatever the priorities, **a deny region always stops new stacks** in the space it covers.

## Who owns a region

A region can have an owner, who sees it even when regions are private and may share it with friends.
See [Private regions]($regions/private_regions).
