# Stacks without AI

Hundreds of mobs standing in a farm spend most of the server's time thinking about where to walk.
`stackAi` decides what a stack of **two or more** mobs does with its AI:

| Value | What the stack does |
|---|---|
| `NORMAL` *(default)* | Thinks like any mob. |
| `PHYSICS_ONLY` | Attacks nothing, follows nobody and stands where it is, but still falls, is carried by water, is knocked back and is **kept afloat** if its kind would swim. Farms built on water streams and drops keep working. |
| `FROZEN` | Like vanilla's `NoAI`: does not move at all, not even to fall. Saves the most. |

```
/mobstacker set stackAi physics_only
/mobstacker region set grinder stackAi frozen     # only in one region
```

- A **single mob always keeps its AI**, so the last mob of a stack wakes up again.
- Nothing is written into the mob. Remove the mod, or set `NORMAL` again, and every mob thinks as
  before.
- A stack without AI still despawns as vanilla would, and a creeper stops swelling.

> [!WARNING]
> A mob that does not think does not do anything else either. A sheep does not eat grass, so its
> wool does not grow back. Villagers do not work or breed. Animals do not follow the food you hold. Use
> it per region where that is what you want, such as a mob grinder.
