# Pets & mounts

## What never stacks

**Anything a player has put something into stays out of stacks**, and there is no setting to change
that:

- tamed or owned mobs,
- saddled mobs, horse armour, a donkey's or llama's chest,
- leashed mobs, and anything riding or being ridden,
- an ocelot that trusts you, a fox that knows you,
- a horse somebody has started to tame,
- an iron golem a player built (since 1.12.0; a village's golems still stack).

A horse's saddle and chest live in an inventory of its own, so a merge would have wiped them along
with the taming; hence the hard rule. Wild herds still stack, and foals are born wild, so a breeding
pen keeps stacking what it produces.

## Taking one animal out

When you interact with a stack, **one animal steps out** and everything you do applies to that one:

- **Mounts** (horses, donkeys, mules, llamas, camels, skeleton and zombie horses): right-click the stack
  and one comes out to be tamed, saddled, fed or ridden.
- **Taming**: hold a bone to a stack of wolves, cod to cats or seeds to parrots, and one steps out. An
  empty hand does nothing, as in vanilla.
- **Buckets**: a bucket takes one fish or axolotl, not the whole school.
- **Shears** with `stackedHarvest` off: one sheep comes out and is sheared (see
  [Breeding, shearing & drops]($gameplay/farming)).

The animal walks back into the herd later unless what you did keeps it out (taming, a saddle, a leash).
`separationCooldown` (seconds, default 0) keeps any mob taken out of a stack, or poured out of a bucket,
away from stacks for a while.

## Every horse keeps its own stats

**Every horse in a stack keeps its own speed, jump strength and health.** The horse that comes out is
the next one in line with its own numbers, and when the top horse dies, the next one takes over with
its own. A good horse merged into a herd is not lost, and a herd merged under a good horse does not
become a herd of good horses.

## The separator item

With `enableSeparator` on, right-clicking a stack with the `separatorItem` (default
`minecraft:diamond`) takes one mob out, using up the item if `consumeSeparator` is on. A mob taken out
this way is named `Lone <mob>`, and named mobs do not stack, so it stays on its own.

| Setting | Default |
|---|---|
| `enableSeparator` | off |
| `separatorItem` | `minecraft:diamond` |
| `consumeSeparator` | on |

## The assimilator item

With `enableAssimilator` on, **sneaking and right-clicking a mob with the `assimilatorItem`** (default
`minecraft:emerald`) switches whether it may stack: off keeps that mob, or the whole stack it is, out of
stacks for good; another click lets it back in. The action bar says which. It uses up the item (or a
point of its durability) if `consumeAssimilator` is on. Sneaking is needed so that trading with a
villager, emeralds in hand, does not switch the villager.

| Setting | Default |
|---|---|
| `enableAssimilator` | off |
| `assimilatorItem` | `minecraft:emerald` |
| `consumeAssimilator` | on |
