# Equipment

Mobs that hold or wear something stay **out of stacks by default** (`stackEquippedMobs` is off), because
gear belongs to one mob and a stack shows only one.

Switch `stackEquippedMobs` on and, with `keepMemberEquipment` (on by default), the stack **remembers
what every member wears and holds**, piece by piece, with each piece's drop chance.

When a member dies, its own gear is put back on the mob just before its loot is rolled, so vanilla does
the rest:

- each piece drops with its own chance, and **Looting** raises it,
- dropped armour is damaged the way vanilla damages it,
- items from **other mods**, and any enchantment, work without the mod knowing anything about them.

What is left of a killed stack, and a mob taken out of one, carry the next member's gear. Without
`keepMemberEquipment` they wear what the stack's top mob spawned with, never what it picked up: that
is the top mob's own and drops when it dies.

| Setting | Effect | Default |
|---|---|---|
| `stackEquippedMobs` | Mobs with gear may stack at all | off |
| `keepMemberEquipment` | Remember each member's gear (needs `stackEquippedMobs`) | on |

> [!NOTE]
> Without `keepMemberEquipment`, a merge keeps only the surviving mob's gear, and what the other mobs
> wore is lost.
