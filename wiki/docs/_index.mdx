# MobStacker: Restacked

**MobStacker: Restacked** merges nearby mobs of the same kind into one entity, so a farm with four
hundred cows costs the server four cows' worth of ticking. Loot, experience, health and behaviour are
kept: the mobs are still there, they just travel together as `Cow x100`.

It runs on **Minecraft 1.20.1**, on **Fabric** (with Fabric API) and on **Forge** (47 or newer, nothing
else needed). Stacking happens on the server; a player only needs the mod for the config screen and for
seeing regions in the world.

> [!IMPORTANT]
> A fresh install stacks **nothing** until you choose where stacking may happen. See
> [Getting started]($getting_started).

## What it does

- **You decide where mobs stack**: everywhere, near players, or only inside regions you draw. A deny
  region keeps a place stack-free in every mode.
- **Almost every setting can differ per region**, so a cow farm and a mob grinder in the same world
  behave differently.
- **Regions can be seen in the world**: coloured boxes with their names, and a line telling you which
  region you stand in. Players see only the regions they own or that were shared with them.
- **Fights stay fair**: a big hit kills several mobs and drops loot for each, Sweeping Edge works on a
  stack, and a fall, fire or lava hurts every mob in a stack, so farms keep working.
- **Nothing is lost**: every stacked mob keeps its own armour and weapons, every horse its own speed,
  and a stack can be split back into its mobs before you remove the mod.
- **Pets are safe**: anything tamed, saddled, leashed or named is left alone, and right-clicking a wild
  herd hands you one animal.
- **Farms scale with the stack**: shearing, milking and breeding a stack give one mob's worth per
  member, and its drops come out as a few full stacks and one experience orb.
- **Fewer things to tick**: stacks can optionally stop thinking (they still fall and float) or freeze.
- **Ask a stack why**: `/mobstacker inspect` names the rule that keeps a mob out of a stack.
